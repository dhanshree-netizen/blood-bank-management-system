Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before donating.

Who can donate blood?

Healthy adults aged 18 and above with valid donor information can participate.

Is the process safe?

Yes. Donations are handled under trusted healthcare guidelines and verified support protocols.

How quickly can requests be matched?

Matches are created instantly based on the availability and blood group of registered donors.
